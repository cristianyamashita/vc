How to play

Build a working production line without exceeding the budget.

1
Choose and place

Choose a part on the right, then click a free grid cell. Drag to paint several conveyors.

2
Point the arrows

Items leave every part in its arrow direction. Rotate before placing with the button, R, or right-click.

3
Process the cargo

An assembler consumes 2 ore and, after 3 cycles, releases 1 gear.

4
Run and deliver

Connect the line to a green dock, press Run, and deliver the target item count.

First plan recipe

Place an assembler in the first cell to the right of the mine. Keep its arrow pointing right, add right-facing conveyors up to the dock, then press Run.

➜ ConveyorMoves one item per cycle in the arrow direction. Cost: 1.
⚙ AssemblerTurns every 2 ore into 1 gear. Cost: 4.
⑂ SplitterAlternates items between forward and clockwise exits. Cost: 3.

R rotate · Space run or pause · Reset stops the simulation but keeps your layout; Clear removes the layout.
