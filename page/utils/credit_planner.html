Help

Track services with monthly credits and see how many credits to use per day before the period ends.

How it works

  • Use New to add a service with its monthly credits, current balance and period end date.
  • Credits per day = current credits ÷ days left in the period.
  • Choose % to enter the balance as a percentage of the monthly credits.
  • The last update turns red when the balance was not updated today; edit the card to refresh it.
  • Data is stored locally in this browser (IndexedDB).
  • Press ? to open this help.
