Eye Gaze Control

Control the screen with your eyes and head. Move your head to aim, blink or dwell to select. No extra hardware — just your webcam.

Note: Camera requires localhost or HTTPS. Keep your head centered. Move slowly at first.

How to use

  1. Face the camera and keep your head centered.
  2. Move your head slowly left/right/up/down to aim.
  3. Blink deliberately (1–2s after aiming) to select.
  4. Use Re-center if the cursor drifts off-target.
